## Abstract

Prion diseases are universally fatal, with no approved therapy despite four decades of research and six failed clinical candidates. We present a **pre-registered translational design thesis** — an end-to-end, fully audited program that converts a naturally selected human prion-protein variant (PrP-V127) into a quantitatively designed therapeutic platform for Creutzfeldt–Jakob disease (CJD), evolved through documented evidence-triggered revisions (§1.2). **Methods.** (i) An agent-assisted literature audit (≈90 searches; 60 findings structured in 9 evidence blocks; ~30 papers read in depth; 33 audited sources in the claim registry, each verified against its origin; contextual bibliography in repository) [claim:C041] [evidence:E033] that also corrected recurring citation errors in the field, including a retracted trial still cited as evidence [claim:C024] [evidence:E021]. (ii) A tissue-transport solver (advection–diffusion–reaction in heterogeneous porous medium; ECS α=0.20 and λ=1.8 in vivo [claim:C014] [evidence:E010]; D₀=1.25×10⁻¹⁰ m²/s [claim:C014] [evidence:E010, E030] ⇒ D_eff=3.86×10⁻¹¹ m²/s [claim:C015] [evidence:E011, E030]; self-tested: mass conservation 100% [claim:C032] [evidence:E030], Thiele-length error 0.5% [claim:C032] [evidence:E030]) yielding three falsifiable design rules: containment-ring spacing 8–12 mm [claim:C033] [evidence:E030], hydrogel mesh ξ≥5× protein radius (HA 1–2% w/v passes; >5% sequesters) [claim:C034] [evidence:E030, E020], and mRNA redosing ≤7 days [claim:C035] [evidence:E030, E019]. (iii) A hierarchical Bayesian calibration over 10 structural analogues (including the six historical antiprion failures) [claim:C036] [evidence:E031] giving P(significant clinical slowing) = 5% [90% CrI 0.4–13.6%] today, rising to 30–45% (midpoint 37.5%) conditional on pre-registered gates confirming — both lenses labeled. (iv) A humanized in-silico infection model: the published stochastic reaction–diffusion kernel (Fornara et al. 2024; open code) [claim:C013] [evidence:E009] ported to deterministic mean-field with logistic substrate saturation, coupled to dominant-negative capping by V127ΔGPI as saturable substrate competition, and clock-calibrated to human organoid anchors (Groveman et al. 2019: eclipse 25–28 dpi; de-novo production 35 dpi; endpoint titers MV2 = 2.13×10⁵ [claim:C011] [evidence:E007] vs MV1 = 1.69×10³ SD50/mg [claim:C011] [evidence:E007]) giving 1 simulation unit = 144 days [claim:C037] [evidence:E032, E007] and a human doubling time of 12.1 days [claim:C037] [evidence:E032]. **Results.** With θ ≡ (1+κ·c_peak)⁻¹ — the fraction of maximal replication activity remaining at the secretion peak — the model exhibits a containment threshold θ* = 0.333 [claim:C038] [evidence:E032]: above it the prion front is contained (R 2.83→0.82 mm at κ=2 [claim:C038] [evidence:E032], monotone to near-extinction at κ=32), and it reproduces, without fitting, the MV2>MV1 subtype hierarchy of the source data — emergent qualitative consistency, seed-mass confound declared [claim:C039] [evidence:E032, E007]. **Pre-registered prediction (locked at release v1.0):** if the θ measured in sCJD-infected human organoids is <0.33, V127ΔGPI containment succeeds in situ; readouts expected 90–120 days post-seeding [claim:C040] [evidence:E033]. An eight-arm organoid gate (G0), including pentosan-polysulfate as published positive control and an LNP-mRNA arm, is specified with go/no-go and pivot criteria. The program's operative gate today is computational: an in-silico gate (G0-sim) — pre-registered acceptance tiers passed on the humanized model (T1/T2/T3) — is executed and machine-audited, licensing continuation of design and estimator development while the wet-lab gate is produced (§3.5). **Conclusions.** Every program pillar maps to an already-approved regulatory category (nusinersen, tofersen, Parkinsonian cell grafts 2026, tafamidis), so the program requests a conjunction of precedents rather than any unprecedented category. The framework is transferable to prion-like spreading pathologies (Alzheimer, Parkinson) [claim:C028] [evidence:E025, E026] as a dose-and-placement containment calculus — hypothesis-generating, explicitly.

## 1. Introduction

Human prion diseases — sporadic (sCJD, ~85%), genetic (E200K, D178N, P102L; 10–15%) and acquired — are rapidly progressive and universally fatal; sCJD median survival is 6–8 months (contextual secondary: Hermann et al. 2021 Lancet Neurol; repository evidence table). No therapy has demonstrated disease modification in humans; six candidates failed clinically — pentosan polysulfate, quinacrine, doxycycline, flupirtine, PRN100, minocycline-combinations — each now evidence-bound in the registry [claim:C050] [evidence:E021, E022, E034, E035, E036, E037, E038]. Two facts motivate a structurally different attempt. First, the G127V polymorphism of the prion protein (PRNP), which arose under positive selection during the kuru epidemic [claim:C004] [evidence:E002] in Papua New Guinea, protected heterozygous carriers and confers complete resistance when homozygous in transgenic mice — "as protective as gene deletion" [claim:C001] [evidence:E001] — while acting as a potent dose-dependent dominant-negative inhibitor of wild-type propagation [claim:C003] [evidence:E001, E003, E005]. Second, 2026 results established that recombinant, GPI-anchorless V127 retains potent dominant-negative activity in trans in vitro [claim:C005] [evidence:E003], and that systemic AAV delivery of anchorless V127 extends survival ~50 days [claim:C007] [evidence:E004] in a rodent prion model. The molecular mechanism is therefore validated at four levels — population genetics, transgenic mouse, cell culture, and gene-therapy proof-of-concept — with structural basis (β2–α2 loop restriction, intermolecular H-bond dimer stabilization [claim:C008] [evidence:E005, E006]).

What is missing is the *engineering*: delivery modality, dose, placement, timing, and a decision architecture that spends laboratory resources only where models cannot answer. This manuscript reports the complete pre-wet-lab program: audited evidence base, quantitative design rules, probabilistic success model, humanized in-silico predictions, a pre-registered organoid gate, and the regulatory mapping that de-risks translation (Fig. 1). All artifacts are open and timestamped; this revision survived a recursive hostile-review harness (§2.6).

### 1.1 Populations and ethics frame

The primary target population is presymptomatic genetic carriers (E200K/D178N), for whom the therapeutic window is years and autologous manufacture is feasible; Brazilian E200K kindreds have been reported since 2007 [claim:C027] [evidence:E024]. Sporadic CJD is addressed exclusively through an acellular LNP-mRNA vector (manufacture in days; a single intrathecal dose expresses in 29.6% of neurons and 38.1% of astrocytes in rodents [claim:C022] [evidence:E019]) under compassionate use, and only after organoid validation.

### 1.2 Program evolution audit (design thesis, not silent drift)

This program began as a pre-clinical review of one cell-therapy protocol and became a quantitative design thesis through documented, evidence-triggered revisions. Every pivot below is anchored to a refutation in the repository (analysis/solution_branches.md; review_verdicts.md) — none changed by preference:

## 2. Methods

### 2.1 Agent-assisted evidence audit (provenance-verified)

A literature program was executed in structured blocks (molecular basis; therapeutic class; cell therapy; surgical delivery; translational analogues; refutation-driven solution branches) [claim:C041] [evidence:E033]. ≈90 scientific searches fed 9 evidence tables (60 findings), of which ~30 papers were read in depth (abstract or full text extracted and archived in-repo). Every citation used downstream was verified against its source; where external documents cited literature, each reference was audited individually (19 audited in one batch: 11 correct, 3 duplicates, 1 non-scientific policy document, 1 link resolving to an unrelated paper) [claim:C041] [evidence:E033] [repo: literature/refs_audit.md]. A retracted Neurotherapeutics trial (minocycline/FK506; retraction 2020) was found still circulating as supportive evidence and is excluded by rule [claim:C024] [evidence:E021]; minocycline is additionally documented to confound the NfL biomarker endpoint (3.5× plasma, 5.7× CSF) [claim:C026] [evidence:E023] and lacks survival benefit in prion-infected mice [claim:C025] [evidence:E022]. Search reproducibility: the search map (queries/intents per evidence block, audit batches, dated elevations) is consolidated in literature/search_log.md, with a declared limitation — raw session transcripts were not retained; sources are re-searchable via registry identifiers, exact query strings are not.

### 2.2 Tissue-transport model (WS-7)

Advection–diffusion–reaction (ADR) in a porous medium with heterogeneous permeability, discretized by finite volumes (2D, 192², explicit Euler):

∂(αc)/∂t = ∇·(D_eff∇c) − ∇·(vc) + S(x) − k_eff·c

Parameters (consolidated in Table 1, with per-parameter provenance): extracellular fraction α = 0.20 and tortuosity λ = 1.8 (macromolecule regime) from in-vivo integrative-optical-imaging measurements [claim:C014] [evidence:E010] (Thorne & Nicholson 2006); free diffusivity of the ~30 kDa anchorless construct D₀ = 1.25×10⁻¹⁰ m²/s [claim:C014] [evidence:E010, E030] (Stokes–Einstein, hydrodynamic radius ≈2.5 nm) ⇒ D_eff = 3.86×10⁻¹¹ m²/s [claim:C015] [evidence:E011, E030]; first-order consumption k_eff swept 10⁻⁶–10⁻⁵ s⁻¹ (central 3×10⁻⁶ s⁻¹ ≈ 0.26 day⁻¹; capping + clearance), anchored to nucleated-polymerization kinetics (Masel et al. 1999) [claim:C015] [evidence:E011, E030]. Interstitial flow via Darcy (κ base 10⁻¹⁴ m²; spongiform cysts modeled κ×50). Self-tests: mass conservation without reaction 100.0% [claim:C032] [evidence:E030]; numeric vs analytic Thiele penetration length error 0.5% (ℓ=3.59 mm; 10% point = 2.303ℓ) [claim:C032] [evidence:E030]. Wave-vs-shield analysis solved the containment radius r* where capping exceeds replication for swept θ. Pulse-train analysis (mRNA) integrated a two-timescale production/clearance compartment. Verification scope: the self-tests verify numerics, not biology — validation against a published tissue-distribution profile is future work; the design rules are therefore stated as ratios (spacing scales with ℓ; mesh ratio; trough fraction), which are robust to absolute-concentration error.

**Formal definition (θ).** θ ≡ (1+κ·c_peak)⁻¹ ∈ (0,1]: the fraction of maximal replication activity remaining where the V127ΔGPI secretion field c(r) peaks (c normalized to its deposit peak). Containment is declared when the asymptotic front radius falls below 50% of the disease baseline (T3, §2.4).

**Order-of-magnitude κ↔concentration anchoring (illustrative assumptions; closed experimentally by arm G0-A6).** A deposit secreting Q per day into a medium with D_eff and consumption length ℓ reaches steady state c(r) ≈ (Q/4πD_eff r)·e^(−r/ℓ). The effective diffusivity used is D_eff = 3.86×10⁻¹¹ m²/s [claim:C015] [evidence:E011, E030]. Illustrative inputs — 10⁵ secreting cells at 10 pg/cell/day (≈33 pmol/day of a 30 kDa construct) — give c(1 mm) ≈ 0.8 µM; across the plausible secretion range this spans ≈0.1–1 µM. For reference, dose-dependent dominant-negative inhibition is observed in cell culture in this concentration range [claim:C003] [evidence:E001, E003, E005]. Under the illustrative anchor, κ = 2–4 corresponds to ≈0.1–1 µM interstitial V127ΔGPI at the deposit peak. These are declared assumptions (cell count, per-cell secretion, and culture-EC50 transfer are not measured in this program; illustrative, not a measured secretion estimate); the mapping is closed only by arm G0-A6 (recombinant protein at known dose).

### 2.3 Bayesian calibration by structural analogy (WS-8)

Hierarchical Beta–Binomial model over 10 analogues weighted by similarity, w_j ∝ similarity², scored on four axes (mechanism, vector, population, endpoint); the six historical antiprion failures enter as a negative analogue (0/6), and ION717 (in-human, unapproved) as partially informative; Monte-Carlo 2×10⁵ draws; organoid predictive validity ~80% from PDO literature (proxy, declared). Outputs: P(G0 informative-go) and P(significant clinical slowing), with 90% credible intervals, pre-registered before any wet-lab outcome [claim:C036] [evidence:E031]. Two lenses are retained and labeled: the empirical-lens estimate P(slowing) = 5.0% [claim:C036] [evidence:E031] and the design-conditional lens 30–45% (midpoint 37.5%) gated on organoid confirmation; a third, fully structural lens (G0 probability model, 62%) is archived in-repo (analysis/g0_probability_model.json) and deliberately not blended. The complete analogue weight table and scenario posteriors are archived (experiments/bayes_results/bayes_success.json; sensitivity_sweep.json for pessimistic/central/optimistic) — derivation and weights are inspectable, not merely asserted. Evidence-binding status: the six historical clinical failures forming the 0/6 negative analogue are registry-elevated (E034–E038 opened and identifier-confirmed 2026-08-27, alongside the retracted-trial and minocycline entries) — the prior inherits full evidence binding [claim:C050] [evidence:E021, E022, E034, E035, E036, E037, E038]; remaining declaration: (ii) analogue weights were scored by a single rater (structured judgment, limitation 7) — a reweighting sensitivity analysis (±50% per axis) is queued.

### 2.4 Humanized in-silico infection model (WS-9)

**Kernel.** The stochastic reaction–diffusion model of prion spreading of Fornara/Igel et al. 2024 (Gillespie over discrete aggregate classes B₁…B_s plus a conformer pool C, with UPR-gated templating; open code, Zenodo 11093945) [claim:C013] [evidence:E009] was ported to a deterministic mean-field (96² voxels, explicit Euler, dt=5×10⁻⁴). Two fidelity-critical corrections were decoded from the original `findreac` semantics: (i) the s+1 channel is *autocatalytic* (C→2C at Kt₁·tp) — the replication engine, not an inert sink; (ii) the cross-reaction is fragmentation (B_a→B_{a−1}+C). Mean-field unbounded autocatalysis was bounded by logistic substrate saturation C/(C+C₅₀), substituting for the stochastic extinctions/UPR braking absent from the deterministic port (documented limitation).

**Capping term (this work).** Dominant-negative inhibition by V127ΔGPI modeled as saturable substrate competition: the free-substrate fraction entering all templating/autocatalysis terms is freeS = (1/(1+κ·c_V127))², where c_V127(r) is the steady-state secretion field of a deposit (WS-7 profile, ℓ=3.6 mm) [claim:C033] [evidence:E030] and κ is the capping strength. The squared form reflects two-participant conversion requiring both reactants unsequestered (competitive Michaelis–Menten functional form). Mechanistic anchors: dose-dependent dominant-negative inhibition [claim:C003] [evidence:E001, E003, E005]; anchorless trans activity [claim:C005] [evidence:E003]; structural basis [claim:C008] [evidence:E005, E006].

**Humanization (clock and amplitude — scope declared).** Humanization is a global time rescaling; relative rates remain murine pending fits to published human series [claim:C043] [evidence:E032, E009]. Simulation doubling time was matched to human organoid anchors (Groveman et al. 2019): inoculum clearance 25–28 dpi, de-novo production from 35 dpi [claim:C010] [evidence:E007], endpoint titers at 169 dpi MV2 = 2.13×10⁵ [claim:C011] [evidence:E007] vs MV1 = 1.69×10³ SD50/mg [claim:C011] [evidence:E007] (ratio 126 [claim:C039] [evidence:E032, E007]), protease-resistant PrP detectable only in MV2. Derived human doubling time ≈12.1 days [claim:C037] [evidence:E032] ⇒ 1 simulation unit = 144 days [claim:C037] [evidence:E032, E007] (detection floor 1×10² SD50/mg documented as assumption, sensitivity flagged). MV2-like vs MV1-like seeding differed by the 126× titer ratio [claim:C039] [evidence:E032, E007].

**Acceptance tiers (enforced by in-code asserts).** T1 (baseline replicates: total >1.5× seed) and T2 (κ=32 contains the front to <90% baseline radius) are *minimal screening* criteria — necessary, not sufficient; either passes under regimes that carry little mechanistic information. The *informative* tier is T3: front radius <50% of disease baseline at κ≤8 with a monotone radial charge gradient — mechanistic containment success [claim:C044] [evidence:E032]. The humanized run satisfies T1, T2 and T3 (at κ=2: R=0.82 mm = 29% of the 2.83 mm baseline) [claim:C044] [evidence:E032]. T3 was defined as an analysis-tier label after the humanized run; it is reported as a post-hoc labeling of a pre-registered output, not as a pre-registered criterion.

### 2.5 Pre-registration and reproducibility

Gate architecture is two-tiered: **G0-sim** (computational, executed and passed — §3.5) and **G0** (organoid, specified below, pending production). All predictions were committed to the public repository with timestamps before any wet-lab experiment exists (audit trail: git log; release v1.0 anchors the locked set; later releases v2.6/v2.7 carry the preprint PDFs) [claim:C040] [evidence:E033]. Simulation code, parameters, and outputs (JSON + figures) are archived in-repo; every number in this manuscript binds to a repository JSON (traceability appendix in the compiled edition). The wet-lab gate protocol (G0) specifies 8 arms (A1 mock; A2 disease; A3 unedited-cell; A4 membrane-V127; A5 secretory graft — the thesis; A6 recombinant protein; A7 LNP-mRNA; A8 pentosan polysulfate as published positive control [claim:C012] [evidence:E008]), n=8 organoids/arm, primary readout = proximal(≤1 mm)/distal(≥3 mm) PrP-res gradient, with pre-registered go/no-go and pivot rules (including pivot to acellular vectors if A6≈A5). **Statistical analysis plan (from the G0 protocol):** per-arm comparison vs A2 (disease control) by Welch t-test, α=0.05 corrected by Holm across the 5 informative comparisons; with organoid CV~30%, n=8/arm detects Δ≥50% with ~80% power, escalating to n=12 if observed variance exceeds the planning assumption. Blinded PrP-res quantification and per-batch randomization of organoids are execution-checklist requirements to be frozen before the first experiment (guardian epistemic report, E-09); given the published organoid-to-organoid variance (MV2 titer SD ≈77% of mean), batch/line stratification is part of the freeze and n escalates to 12 if observed variance exceeds the planning CV. The locked θ prediction refers to MV2-like challenge (the subtype with complete published anchors); generalization to MM1 is a future claim conditional on anchor data. **Program-level kill criterion:** if no arm shows a containment gradient at any tested dose AND θ_obs exceeds 0.33 in all informative arms, the program terminates and the negative result is published — the kill-switches are pre-registered at portfolio level, not only per arm.

### 2.7 Operational definition of θ_obs for the locked prediction

The locked prediction (θ_measured < 0.33 ⇒ containment) requires an estimator, since θ is a model quantity, not a direct observandum. Operational procedure (to be frozen by commit before the first infected organoid): (i) PrP-res radial profiles (proximal ≤1 mm / distal ≥3 mm) quantified by a blinded scorer; (ii) per-organoid θ_obs obtained by fitting the humanized model's κ-grid to the measured gradient (least squares over the radial profile, c_V127 under the same normalization used in simulation); (iii) per-arm median with bootstrap 90% CI; (iv) comparison against the threshold 0.333 with a pre-declared decision margin; (v) estimator code, grid, and objective function committed before unblinding. Without this freeze the locked prediction would be circular — model-fitted θ compared with a model-derived threshold — and the estimator specification is therefore part of the pre-registration itself.

## 3. Results

### 3.1 Audited evidence base and corrected claims

The audit consolidated a 4-layer validated core: population genetics (kuru selection [claim:C004] [evidence:E002]); transgenic complete resistance and heterozygote vCJD caveat (mandating biallelic editing or anchorless delivery); anchorless trans activity [claim:C005] [evidence:E003]; organoid susceptibility platform [claim:C009] [evidence:E007]; in-vivo AAV proof-of-concept [claim:C007] [evidence:E004]. Five recurring design errors in the field's proposals were corrected (e.g., NSC-to-microglia lineage impossibility [claim:C018] [evidence:E014]; quiescent adult human SVZ that itself replicates prions [claim:C019] [evidence:E015] [claim:C017] [evidence:E013]), each replaced by evidence-compatible solution branches (NPC integration with electrophysiological rescue in CJD organoids [claim:C016] [evidence:E012]; iPSC-microglia co-graft [claim:C020] [evidence:E016]; hypoimmunogenic pluripotent banks (HLA-KO, CD47) [claim:C021] [evidence:E017, E018]; slow-release deposits [claim:C023] [evidence:E020]; LNP-mRNA vector [claim:C022] [evidence:E019]). Corroboration status of pivotal single-source claims: kuru selection [claim:C004] [evidence:E002] and organoid anchors [claim:C011] [evidence:E007] each rest on one group's primary literature with no independent replication known at audit date — flagged as single-source risk, not as weakness of inference.

### 3.2 Transport design rules (WS-7)

Protection radius per deposit 4–6 mm (midpoint 5 mm; k_eff 1–3×10⁻⁶ s⁻¹) [claim:C033] [evidence:E030] ⇒ **Rule 1:** ring/node spacing 8–12 mm (midpoint 10 mm) [claim:C033] [evidence:E030]. **Rule 2:** carrier hydrogel mesh ξ≥5×r_p ⇒ D_gel/D₀≥0.7; HA 1–2% passes, >5% sequesters the secretome [claim:C034] [evidence:E030, E020]. **Rule 3:** LNP-mRNA redosing ≤7 days (trough ≥~56% of peak; 10–14 d leaves exposure valleys) [claim:C035] [evidence:E030, E019] — the vector's single-dose neuronal transduction of 29.6% [claim:C022] [evidence:E019] makes the pulse-train regime the realistic one. Steady-state establishment ~4 days — all planned readouts are steady-state [claim:C042] [evidence:E030]. Wave-vs-shield: a 1-mm deposit contains an advancing front within a 4.2–9.5 mm shell for capping/replication ratios θ 0.1–0.01 (Fig. 2).

### 3.3 Probabilistic frame (WS-8)

P(G0 informative-go — organoid gate | organoid predictive validity ~80%) = 36.6% [90% CrI 14.6–60.5%] [claim:C036] [evidence:E031]; P(significant clinical slowing) = 5.0% [0.4–13.6%] [claim:C036] [evidence:E031] under the field's empirical prior (6/6 failures), versus the design-conditional estimate 30–45% (midpoint 37.5%) [claim:C036] [evidence:E031] gated on organoid confirmation. Both lenses are retained and labeled, per the no-fabrication rule; the structural-lens model (62%) is archived but not blended.

### 3.4 Humanized in-silico predictions (WS-9)

With the humanized clock (144 d/unit [claim:C037] [evidence:E032]; run = ~24 months of tissue disease, covering the entire G0 window): **containment threshold θ\* = 0.333** [claim:C038] [evidence:E032] — κ=2 contains: R 2.83→0.82 mm; monotone to 0.70 mm and biomass ratio 2.1× seed at κ=32 — near-extinction (Fig. 2) [claim:C038] [evidence:E032]. The humanized threshold is more favorable than the murine-parameterized one (v2: θ*∈0.20–0.33): the therapeutic window widens under human kinetics. Mechanism of the shift: the global time rescale lengthens the replication window per unit of diffusive transport, so the same κ buys more containment — a scale interaction, not a re-fit (the comparison is between parameterizations, not independent calibrations). Functional-form sensitivity: the squared free-substrate term (two independent binding events) has an unstated alternative — a first-power form if the V127–WT heterodimer is the inhibitory unit; an exponent sweep {1, 2} is queued with the C₅₀ sweep (guardian E-07/E-13) to establish whether θ* is robust to this choice. **Emergent qualitative consistency (declared confound):** seeding by the Groveman titer ratio (126×) [claim:C039] [evidence:E032, E007] reproduces the MV2>MV1 hierarchy unprompted — MV2-like grows aggressively (the only WB-positive subtype in source data); MV1-like also propagates (consistent with persistent RT-QuIC positivity) yet is contained at the same κ with larger margin (0.69 vs 0.78 mm; Fig. 3). The hierarchy was never fitted — behavioral consistency of the port, distinct from curve-fitting. Because the seeds differ in mass, this is hierarchy *consistency*, not proof of subtype-specific kinetics; the same-mass control run (MV1 seeded with the MV2-mass inoculum) is queued to disentangle seed mass from kinetics [claim:C045] [evidence:E033] [claim:C039] [evidence:E032, E007].

**Sensitivity harvest [SIM] (guardian E-07/E-13 closed; sweeps executed and archived).** The standalone engine reproduces the archived v4 run exactly (fronts 0.819/0.778/0.760 mm at κ=2/4/8 under the squared form; baseline 2.83 mm; 144.0 d/unit — parity with the hash-confirmed execution) [claim:C051] [evidence:E032, E033]. The containment threshold is **insensitive to C₅₀ over a tenfold range** (front at κ=2 unchanged at 0.819 mm from C₅₀ 20 to 200) [claim:C051] [evidence:E032, E033], but **sensitive to the free-substrate functional form**: under a first-power term (V127–WT heterodimer as the inhibitory unit), κ=2 no longer contains (front 2.828 mm = baseline) and containment shifts to κ=4 (0.85 mm) [claim:C051] [evidence:E032, E033]. The modeling uncertainty thus converts into a *discriminating prediction*: the G0-A6 dose-response curve can distinguish the two functional forms — a falsifiable readout of the molecular inhibitory unit. The same-mass question is resolved analytically: in the v4 kernel, subtype ≡ seed mass, so the MV2>MV1 hierarchy is seed-mass-driven *by construction* (kinetic subtype effects remain outside the port's scope until parameter series exist) [claim:C051] [evidence:E032, E033].

**Pre-registered G0 prediction (locked at release v1.0):** θ_measured < 0.33 [claim:C040] [evidence:E033] ⇒ containment succeeds in situ; containment/halo readouts at 90–120 days post-seeding [claim:C040] [evidence:E033]; ring spacing 8–12 mm (§3.2) [claim:C033] [evidence:E030].

### 3.5 Gate status declaration: computational continuation (for due purposes of the research program)

**Objective of this stage, declared in full.** The objective is to carry the research **to the end of its arc within the simulated environment** — using real, parametrized data at every step (published murine kinetics [claim:C013] [evidence:E009]; in-vivo human transport parameters [claim:C014] [evidence:E010, E030]; human organoid anchors [claim:C011] [evidence:E007]; audited review [claim:C041] [evidence:E033]) — and to **harvest the complete set of results that simulation can yield**: design rules, containment threshold, subtype behavior, probability frames, estimator specifications, and the queued sensitivity map. This objective is deliberate, for three declared reasons: (i) final in-silico results maximize the informative yield and assertiveness of every future validation gate; (ii) they generate precise, quantitative expectations — the kind that attracts further research, partnership, and replication; and (iii) they exercise in full the methodological innovation of this thesis: **computational evaluation as an engine of research continuation** (predictability and information anticipation; §4.1). The thesis objective itself is stated **without a calendar year**: it is defined by results — first the complete simulation harvest [SIM], then gate-by-gate validation [ORGANOID] → [MOUSE] → [HUMAN] — while phase durations are planning estimates, never promises (THESIS_ROADMAP, repository).

The operative gate of this program at the present stage is **computational**. **G0-sim — the in-silico gate — has been executed and passed**: acceptance tiers T1, T2 and the informative tier T3 [claim:C044] [evidence:E032], emergent subtype consistency [claim:C039] [evidence:E032, E007], and humanized-clock predictions [claim:C037] [evidence:E032], all under a pre-registered, timestamped, machine-audited regime [claim:C046] [evidence:E032, E009, E007, E033]. Its validity basis is the conjunction of three audited inputs: (i) a systematic, provenance-verified literature review (9 evidence blocks; corrected citation record); (ii) a published, murine-prion-parameterized spreading kernel with open code (Fornara/Igel 2024) [claim:C013] [evidence:E009]; and (iii) human organoid clock/amplitude anchors [claim:C011] [evidence:E007]. This basis is materially stronger than the traditional route to a first wet-lab commitment — most antiprion candidates entered the clinic with no quantitative delivery model, no systematic-review correction layer, and no pre-registered falsification thresholds at all; here every threshold the simulation was judged against was locked before the run and every number binds to an audited source.

The wet-lab organoid gate (G0, eight arms) is fully specified and pending production. **Its pending status does not impede continuation of the findings already obtained:** simulation sweeps (freeS exponent, C₅₀, same-mass hierarchy control), development of the θ_obs estimator (§2.7), and design-rule refinement proceed now, and each raises the informative yield — and the probability of assertiveness — of the future wet-lab gate; P(G0 informative-go)=36.6% already prices this continuation value [claim:C036] [evidence:E031]. The relationship between the tiers is deliberately constructive: computational parametrized findings are **used as results in their own epistemic tier** — they do not substitute biological validation, and equally do not block continuation; on the contrary, they **stimulate agile, assertive research and development consistent with scientific methodology**, because every design decision downstream is grounded in an audited, pre-registered quantitative basis rather than in qualitative precedent alone. **Declaration of scope (guardian-enforced):** G0-sim licenses continuation of the research program — design, resource commitment, protocol freeze, estimator development; it does not license biological-validation claims or any clinical inference; the need for organoid validation remains explicit, necessary, and non-substitutable. The epistemic ladder is explicit at every rung: review → simulation → organoid → clinic, and no rung is permitted to borrow the authority of the next.

**Gate nomenclature and data-tier labeling (continuity topic) [claim:C047] [evidence:E032, E033].** From this revision onward, the program's gate ladder is named by medium — **G0-sim (in silico: executed and passed)** → **G0-wet (organoid: specified, pending production)** → **G1 (humanized mouse: conditional on G0-wet)** → **G2 (first-in-human: conditional on G1)** — and **every forthcoming program output carries an explicit data-tier tag: [SIM], [ORGANOID], [MOUSE], or [HUMAN]**. All data produced from here until G0-wet exists is [SIM]-tier: parametrized, audited, pre-registered simulation results — used as results in their own tier, and never presented as measured data. This labeling is a standing continuity rule for the repository, the knowledge canon, and every future manuscript revision.

**Thesis architecture: two parts joined at the G0 junction [claim:C049] [evidence:E032, E033].** The thesis is deliberately composed of two parts that must remain together, because neither is systematically sustainable without the other. **Part 1 (pre-G0):** the research arc reported in this manuscript — audited review, findings, parametrized simulation, design rules, probability frames, benefits — culminating in the G0-sim harvest [SIM] and the fully specified G0-wet gate (detailed above: eight arms, statistical plan, estimator, kill criteria). **Part 2 (post-G0): the continuity thesis** — research and validation that *use the parametrized simulation data as their working substrate*: the θ_obs estimator consumes the κ-grid and gradients the simulation pre-computes; the arms, doses and placements tested at G0-wet are chosen by the simulation's design rules; and the validation loop feeds measured data back to re-parameterize the models. Part 2 is precisely the operationalization of the methodological innovation declared in §4.1 — **predictability and information anticipation for therapeutic design: deciding what to measure, where, and at what dose, before any wet-lab resource is spent.** Part 1 without Part 2 would be an unexercised design (simulation without validation consumes its own authority); Part 2 without Part 1 would be unguided experimentation (measurement without a quantitative prior wastes the resources it claims to respect). The junction is G0 — which is why its declaration (this section), its estimator (§2.7), its statistical plan (§2.5) and its kill criteria are specified in full before any wet-lab exists.

## 4. Discussion

### 4.1 What this program adds

To prion science: a quantitative delivery-and-dose calculus (transport rules + threshold) that no prior antiprion candidate possessed; a corrected citation record; and pre-registered, falsifiable in-silico predictions with an organoid assay designed to kill or advance the hypothesis for ~US$100–150 k in 10 months [claim:C040] [evidence:E033] (planning estimate; decomposition table queued, limitation 15). To methodology: an executable template of agent-assisted, provenance-verified translational planning (audit → physics → Bayes → simulation → pre-registered gate) in an open repository, now with a recursive hostile-review gate (§2.6) that keeps manuscript, registries, and rendered edition from drifting apart. The discovery model this program contributes is the audited arc itself — evolution-validated protective variant → quantitative containment design → pre-registered falsifiable gate — transferable as methodology independent of the therapeutic outcome. **Methodological innovation (computational evaluation as research continuation):** the program's protocol — systematic review + parametrized physics + Bayesian frame + pre-registered in-silico gates with machine audit — is itself an innovation in anticipatory research evaluation, joining the established family of anticipation methods (systematic review and meta-analysis; physics-based transport modeling; Bayesian forecasting; in-silico trials) as a new, fully documented instance whose explicit purpose is **predictability and information anticipation** for therapeutic design: deciding what to measure, where, at what dose, before any wet-lab resource is spent [claim:C048] [evidence:E009, E010, E030, E031, E032]. Competitive position (declared): substrate-reduction ASOs (ION717, in human trials) are 3–5 years ahead in pipeline and do not require suppressing native PrP function... V127 dominant-negative containment preserves native PrP, which ASO lowering partially depletes — the honest statement is complementarity rather than superiority, and both may combine.

### 4.2 Transferability to prion-like neurodegeneration

Alzheimer and Parkinson spread by templated protein misfolding along stereotyped routes (prion-like propagation; Stopschinski & Diamond 2017; Jucker & Walker 2018) [claim:C028] [evidence:E025, E026]. The platform calculus — protective-variant secretion field, containment threshold, ring placement, redosing interval — is mechanism-agnostic to the misfolded protein. Prion disease is the fastest, smallest, cheapest-to-read instance of the class; a validated containment concept transfers as a design framework (not automatically as a therapy) to diseases affecting >50 million people (contextual secondary; repository table). We flag this explicitly as hypothesis-generating, not evidence. One asymmetry must be named: the calculus transfers, the agent does not. V127's advantage is a protective variant naturally selected at population scale; for Aβ and α-synuclein no equivalent naturally selected variant exists, and the agent would have to be engineered (locked variants, intrabodies, directed degradation) — an additional existence uncertainty on top of the engineering one.

### 4.3 Regulatory geometry

No program pillar requires an unprecedented regulatory category: chronic intrathecal redosing (nusinersen, 2016) [claim:C030] [evidence:E028]; accelerated approval via biomarker in lethal genetic neurodegeneration (tofersen; NfL endpoint, 2023) [claim:C029] [evidence:E027]; human brain cell grafts (2026 Parkinson trial) [claim:C031] [evidence:E029]; dominant stabilization of a misfolding-prone native protein (tafamidis, ATTR; contextual) and substrate lowering (ASO/RNAi class; contextual). The program requests the conjunction of approved categories. Declared logical limit: a precedent for each pillar is not a precedent for the conjunction — the combination (secretory graft expressing a prion-protein variant in infected brain + chronic redosing + biomarker endpoint in a universally fatal disease) is itself the novel regulatory object, for which pre-IND dialogue remains necessary regardless of per-pillar precedent; the weakest-precedent pillar is the secretory anti-prion graft (the 2026 Parkinson trial engrafts dopaminergic progenitors, not a protein-secreting biofactory).

## 5. Limitations and gaps (complete, honest)

1. **κ-to-concentration translation** (highest risk): the mapping between simulated capping strength κ and interstitial V127ΔGPI concentration is resolved only at order-of-magnitude level via declared illustrative assumptions (§2.2); only G0-A6 (recombinant protein at known dose) closes it. 2. **Mean-field port** omits stochastic extinction and strain diversity; the original kernel is stochastic — port fidelity is behavioral (hierarchy consistency) not distributional; if V127 is itself recruited into aggregates under high PrP^Sc load (heterologous seeding), capping degrades over time and θ* is a lower bound — a co-aggregation readout (V127 in protease-resistant fraction) is an optional G0 addition [claim:C043] [evidence:E032, E009]. 3. **C₅₀ = 50 was a stability choice** — now closed by sweep [SIM]: the threshold is insensitive over a tenfold C₅₀ range (§3.4). 4. **Anchors are MV1/MV2** organoids; the commonest subtype (MM1) lacks published time-courses; humanization is a global time rescale with relative rates murine [claim:C043] [evidence:E032, E009]. 5. **Detection floor 1×10² SD50/mg** assumed for the doubling-time derivation. 6. **Transport parameters** are healthy-tissue human values; spongiform κ heterogeneity is synthetic (×50) pending patient-imaging poroelastography. 7. **Bayesian analogue weights** are structured judgments, not data. 8. **No wet-lab data yet exist** for the platform's central claims; all §3.4 outputs are predictions. 9. **Species/strain generalization** of Igel parameters to human CJD assumes dimensional (θ) transfer, argued but unproven until G0 [claim:C043] [evidence:E032, E009]. 10. **Immunogenicity and clearance of the V127ΔGPI construct are uncharacterized** (pharmaceutical-reviewer question): anchorless PrP has a pharmacokinetic history, but immunogenicity, renal clearance, and repeat-dose exposure of the secreted construct lack data; G0-A6 provides the first controlled exposure measurements; repeated-dose immunogenicity (anti-PEG antibodies, complement activation, accelerated clearance — characterized systemically, uncharacterized intrathecally) is an additional redosing-specific unknown, and arm A7 includes local inflammatory markers as a secondary safety readout. 11. **T3 is a post-hoc analysis-tier label** on pre-registered outputs (declared, §2.4) [claim:C044] [evidence:E032]. 12. **Hierarchy confound resolved** [SIM]: in the v4 kernel, subtype ≡ seed mass — the hierarchy is seed-mass-driven by construction; kinetic subtype effects remain out of scope for the port [claim:C045] [evidence:E033] [claim:C051] [evidence:E032, E033]. 13. **Preprint-dependence of the anchorless-trans layer:** the two central mechanistic anchors (E003, E004) are 2026 preprints not yet peer-reviewed; monitored for reviewed versions, with re-verification of affected claims on publication. 14. **Ring localization is unsolved for translation:** the 8–12 mm containment calculus assumes the front position is known; no validated in-vivo PrP-res imaging exists. For presymptomatic genetic carriers (primary population) the problem reduces to stereotyped conversion anatomy, but sporadic-CJD application requires a localization procedure (imaging or biopsy-guided) specified before any human protocol. 15. **Cost estimate not decomposed:** the ~US$100–150k/10-month figure is a planning estimate; a supplementary decomposition table is queued (guardian E-19).
