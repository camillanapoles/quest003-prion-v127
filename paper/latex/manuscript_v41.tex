\begin{abstract}\small
Prion diseases are universally fatal with no approved therapy. We report an end-to-end preclinical program converting the naturally selected human variant PrP-V127 into a quantitatively designed therapeutic platform. \textbf{Methods:} an agent-assisted, provenance-verified literature audit ($\sim$90 searches, 60 findings, $\sim$30 papers read in depth; recurring citation errors corrected, a retracted trial excluded); a self-tested advection--diffusion--reaction transport solver (mass conservation 100\%, Thiele error 0.5\%) yielding three design rules (ring spacing 8--12\,mm; hydrogel mesh $\xi\ge5r_p$; mRNA redosing $\le$7\,d); a hierarchical Bayesian frame over 10 structural analogues (including six historical antiprion failures): P(slowing)=5\% [90\% CrI 0.4--13.6] vs 30--45\% conditional on gates; and a humanized in-silico infection model --- the open stochastic kernel of \citet{fornara2024}, ported with fidelity-corrected autocatalysis and fragmentation, capped by V127$\Delta$GPI as saturable substrate competition, clock-calibrated to human organoid anchors \citep{groveman2019} (1 unit\,=\,144 days). \textbf{Results:} containment threshold $\theta^{*}=0.333$ (front 2.83$\to$0.82\,mm at $\kappa=2$; near-extinction at $\kappa=32$) and unprompted reproduction of the MV2$>$MV1 subtype hierarchy (emergent consistency, not curve-fitting). \textbf{Pre-registered prediction (locked at release v1.0):} $\theta_{\text{measured}}<0.33$ in infected organoids $\Rightarrow$ containment in situ; readouts 90--120\,d. An eight-arm organoid gate with published positive control and pivot rules is specified. Every pillar maps to an approved regulatory category; the framework transfers as a design calculus to prion-like neurodegeneration.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Human prion diseases are rapidly progressive and universally fatal; sporadic CJD (sCJD) survival is 6--8 months \citep{hermann2021}. Six candidates failed clinically \citep{groveman2021pps,mead2022,cheng2015}. The G127V polymorphism, positively selected during the kuru epidemic \citep{mead2009}, confers complete resistance in homozygous transgenic mice --- ``as protective as gene deletion'' --- with dose-dependent dominant-negative inhibition of wild-type propagation \citep{asante2015}; heterozygotes remain vCJD-susceptible, mandating biallelic or anchorless designs \citep{asante2015}. In 2026, recombinant anchorless V127 retained trans dominant-negative activity \citep{gatdula2026} and systemic AAV delivery extended survival $\sim$50 days in rodents \citep{zerbes2026}, with structural basis \citep{hosszu2020,zheng2018}. The mechanism is validated at four levels; the \emph{engineering} of delivery, dose and placement is not. This manuscript supplies that engineering, fully audited, before any wet-lab euro is spent. Primary population: presymptomatic genetic carriers (Brazilian E200K kindreds documented since 2007 \citep{smid2007}); sporadic disease only via a post-validation acellular mRNA route \citep{xue2025}.

\section{Methods}
\subsection{Agent-assisted audit}
Structured blocks ($\approx$90 searches $\to$ 9 evidence tables, 60 findings; $\sim$30 papers read in depth). External documents' references were audited individually (one batch of 19: 11 correct, 3 duplicates, 1 non-scientific, 1 wrong link). A retracted Neurotherapeutics trial still cited as evidence is excluded by rule \citep{shahretract}; minocycline also confounds the NfL endpoint \citep{gentile2024}.

\subsection{Transport solver (WS-7)}
ADR in heterogeneous porous medium, finite volumes (2D, $192^2$, explicit Euler):
\begin{equation}
\partial(\alpha c)/\partial t=\nabla\!\cdot\!(D_{\text{eff}}\nabla c)-\nabla\!\cdot\!(\mathbf{v}c)+S(\mathbf{x})-k_{\text{eff}}c
\end{equation}
$\alpha=0.20$, $\lambda=1.8$ from in-vivo integrative optical imaging \citep{thorne2006}; $D_0=1.25\times10^{-10}$\,m$^2$/s (Stokes--Einstein, $R_h\approx2.5$\,nm, $\sim$30\,kDa) $\Rightarrow D_{\text{eff}}\approx3.9\times10^{-11}$\,m$^2$/s; $k_{\text{eff}}$ swept $10^{-6}$--$10^{-5}$\,s$^{-1}$ anchored to nucleated polymerization \citep{masel1999}; Darcy flow with spongiform cysts at $\kappa\times50$. Self-tests: mass conservation 100.0\%; numeric vs analytic Thiele length 0.5\%. Wave-vs-shield solved $r^{*}(\theta)$; mRNA pulse-train integrated.

\emph{Order-of-magnitude $\kappa$-anchoring (new, addresses M1):} a $10^5$-cell deposit secreting 10 pg/cell/day $\approx$ 33 pmol/day; with $D_{\text{eff}}=3.9\times10^{-11}$ and $k=3\times10^{-6}$\,s$^{-1}$ the steady peak is $\sim$0.1--1\,\textmu M within 1\,mm --- exactly the range where anchored recombinant V127 shows half-maximal dominant-negative effect in cell culture \citep{gatdula2026}. $\kappa=2$--4 therefore corresponds to roughly 0.1--1\,\textmu M interstitial V127$\Delta$GPI. This estimate is order-of-magnitude only and is closed experimentally by arm A6 (protein at known dose).

\subsection{Bayesian frame (WS-8)}
Hierarchical Beta--Binomial over 10 analogues weighted by similarity (mechanism, vector, population, endpoint); the six antiprion failures enter as a negative analogue; ION717 as partially informative; organoid predictive validity $\approx$0.8 from PDO cohorts; $2\times10^5$ Monte-Carlo draws. P(G0 informative-go) integrates the mechanism prior with assay validity as $P(\text{go})=\sum_i w_i\,p_i$ (weights in repository \texttt{bayes\_success.json}): 36.6\% [90\% CrI 14.6--60.5]; P(clinical slowing) 5.0\% [0.4--13.6] under the empirical prior \emph{vs} 30--45\% design-conditional --- both lenses retained (no-fabrication rule).

\subsection{Humanized in-silico model (WS-9)}
\emph{Kernel.} The stochastic reaction--diffusion model of \citet{fornara2024} (Gillespie over aggregate classes $B_1..B_s$ plus conformer pool $C$; UPR-gated templating; open code, Zenodo 11093945) ported to deterministic mean-field (96$^2$, $dt=5\times10^{-4}$). Two fidelity-critical corrections decoded from \texttt{findreac}: the $s{+}1$ channel is autocatalytic ($C\to2C$, $K_{t1}\,\cdot$tp) --- the replication engine, not an inert sink --- and the cross-reaction is fragmentation ($B_a\to B_{a-1}+C$). Unbounded mean-field autocatalysis is braked by logistic substrate saturation $C/(C+C_{50})$, substituting stochastic extinction/UPR braking (documented port limitation).
\emph{Capping (this work).} Dominant-negative V127$\Delta$GPI as saturable substrate competition: free-substrate fraction entering all templating/autocatalysis terms
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{freeS}=\big(1/(1+\kappa\,c_{V127}(\mathbf{r}))\big)^{2},
\end{equation}
$c_{V127}(\mathbf{r})$ = steady secretion field of a deposit ($\ell=3.6$\,mm profile from WS-7). Quadratic form: two-participant conversion requires both reactants unsequestered (competitive Michaelis--Menten functional form), anchored mechanistically in \citet{asante2015,gatdula2026,hosszu2020}.
\emph{Humanization.} Clock matched to human organoid anchors \citep{groveman2019}: inoculum clearance 25--28 dpi; de-novo production from 35 dpi; endpoint titers (169 dpi) MV2\,=\,2.13\,(\pm1.63)\,$\times10^{5}$ vs MV1\,=\,1.69\,(\pm0.70)\,$\times10^{3}$\,SD50/mg; protease-resistant PrP only in MV2. Derived human doubling $\approx$12.1\,d $\Rightarrow$ 1 sim unit\,=\,144\,d (detection floor $10^2$ documented). \emph{Humanization is a global time rescaling; relative rates remain murine} (full rate fitting to published MV1/MV2 series is future work; MM1 series unpublished). MV1/MV2-like seeds differ by the 126$\times$ titer ratio.
\emph{Acceptance tiers (revised per review):} T1/T2 = minimal screening (baseline replicates; $\kappa{=}32$ contains). \textbf{T3 = informative success: front $<$50\% baseline radius at $\kappa\le8$ with monotone radial load gradient} --- satisfied by the reported runs.

\subsection{Pre-registration}
All predictions locked at repository release v1.0 (commit \texttt{a95f33c}; commit-level timestamps in \texttt{git log}) before any wet-lab data exist. Code, parameters, outputs (JSON + figures) archived in-repo. The G0 gate specifies 8 arms (mock; disease; unedited cell; membrane-V127; \emph{secretory graft --- thesis}; recombinant protein; LNP-mRNA; pentosan-polysulfate positive control \citep{groveman2021pps}), $n=8$/arm, primary readout proximal($\le$1\,mm)/distal($\ge$3\,mm) PrP-res gradient, with go/no-go and pivot rules (pivot to acellular vectors if A6$\approx$A5).

\section{Results}
\subsection{Audit}
Four-layer validated core (population genetics; complete resistance + heterozygote caveat; anchorless trans activity with post-expression persistence \citep{gatdula2026}; AAV proof-of-concept \citep{zerbes2026}). Five recurring design errors corrected (e.g., NSC$\to$microglia lineage impossibility \citep{ginhoux2010}; quiescent prion-replicating SVZ \citep{sorrells2018,relano2013}), each replaced by evidence-compatible branches (iPSC-microglia co-graft \citep{abud2017}; slow-release deposits; LNP-mRNA \citep{xue2025}).

\subsection{Design rules} $r_{10\%}$=4--6\,mm per deposit $\Rightarrow$ \textbf{R1} spacing 8--12\,mm; \textbf{R2} hydrogel $\xi\ge5r_p$ (HA 1--2\% passes, $>$5\% sequesters); \textbf{R3} redosing $\le$7\,d (trough $\ge$56\% peak); steady state $\sim$4\,d. Wave-vs-shield: a 1\,mm deposit contains the front within 4.2--9.5\,mm for $\theta$ 0.1--0.01.

\subsection{Probabilistic frame} As §2.3: P(G0) 36.6\% [14.6--60.5]; P(slowing) 5\% [0.4--13.6] empirical \emph{vs} 30--45\% gate-conditional.

\subsection{Humanized thresholds (T3-satisfying)} $\theta^{*}=0.333$: $\kappa{=}2$ contains (2.83$\to$0.82\,mm), monotone to near-extinction at $\kappa{=}32$ (0.70\,mm; biomass 2.1$\times$ seed). One run $=$ 24 months tissue-disease --- covers the whole G0 window. Humanized threshold is \emph{more favorable} than murine-parameterized (v2 $\theta^*\in$0.20--0.33). \emph{Emergent consistency (carefully claimed, M3):} seeding by the published titer ratio reproduces the MV2$>$MV1 order unprompted; because the seeds differ in mass, this is \emph{consistency of hierarchy}, not proof of subtype-specific kinetics --- a same-mass MV1-vs-MV2 control is queued. \textbf{Locked prediction:} $\theta_{\text{measured}}<0.33\Rightarrow$ containment; readouts 90--120\,d.

\section{Discussion}
\emph{Contribution.} A dose-and-placement calculus no prior antiprion candidate possessed; a corrected citation record; falsifiable pre-registered predictions with a 10-month, $\sim$\$100--150k kill-or-advance assay; and an executable template of agent-assisted, provenance-verified translational planning.
\emph{Transferability (hypothesis-generating).} Alzheimer and Parkinson spread by templated misfolding along stereotyped routes \citep{stopschinski2017,jucker2018}; the platform calculus --- protective-variant secretion field, containment threshold, ring placement, redosing --- is protein-agnostic. Prion disease is the fastest, cheapest-to-read instance of the class; a validated containment concept transfers as a design framework to diseases of $>$50 million people.
\emph{Regulatory geometry.} Chronic intrathecal redosing (nusinersen); accelerated approval by biomarker in lethal genetic neurodegeneration (tofersen, NfL); human brain cell grafts (2026 Parkinson trial); dominant native-state stabilization (tafamidis); substrate lowering (ASO/RNAi). The program requests the conjunction of approved categories.

\section{Limitations}
(1) $\kappa\to$concentration translation is order-of-magnitude only (\S2.2); closed by G0-A6. (2) Mean-field port omits stochastic extinction/strain diversity; behavioral, not distributional, fidelity. (3) $C_{50}$ stability choice, sensitivity flagged. (4) Anchors are MV1/MV2; MM1 unpublished; \emph{humanization is a global time rescale, rates remain murine}. (5) Detection floor $10^2$ assumed. (6) Transport parameters healthy-tissue; synthetic cyst $\kappa\times50$. (7) Analogue weights structured judgment. (8) \emph{No wet-lab data yet --- all in-silico outputs are predictions.} (9) Species/strain transfer of rates assumed via dimensional $\theta$. (10) Immunogenicity/clearance of the anchorless construct uncharacterized (renal clearance history of anchorless PrP; G0-A6 provides first exposure data).
