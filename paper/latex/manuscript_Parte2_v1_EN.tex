\begin{abstract}\small
Part 1 demonstrated the design thesis (V127 platform, $\theta^{*}{=}0{,}333$, reproduced in two environments). Part 2 — this manifest — is \textbf{the realized thesis and formalizes the research method}: \textbf{ACP, Parameterized Computational Anticipation}, steps P0--P6 (identification $\to$ parametrization $\to$ execution $\to$ harvest $\to$ locked prognosis $\to$ immediate derived research $\to$ optional confrontation). \textbf{Mandatory axis:} everything here is simulation and is declared as such; we do not promise to apply or validate — if real data ever prove analogous, the subsequent steps are already taken (anticipation \emph{banked}). \textbf{Validity Basis is mandatory} (BLOCKED check): simulation does not replace the laboratory — essential for real absorption — and every datum has full lineage (who $\to$ species $\to$ cross-validation $\to$ code $\to$ parametrization $\to$ result). The thesis results are the executed [SIM] findings, including the calibrated $\theta_{\text{obs}}$ estimator and the finding that biomass ratio carries the information the saturated radius loses. \textbf{[NO YEAR]:} simulation prognoses operate in future time by nature. \textbf{Method, not selection:} no laboratory is chosen.
\end{abstract}
\section{The named method: ACP}
Today it is possible to \textbf{continue research by simulation}: validated published data as input, auditable deterministic execution, prognoses locked before any measurement — without replacing or waiting for the laboratory. ACP is a sibling of meta-analysis, physics-based modeling and in-silico trials, and is distinguished by \textbf{locking the prognosis before measurement} and \textbf{labeling simulation in every output} (tiers).

\section{Validity Basis (mandatory)}
\textbf{Triad:} (i) simulation-based thesis \textbf{does not replace} laboratory validation — the laboratory is \textbf{essential} for real absorption; (ii) continuation comes from prognoses: future laboratory exams \emph{equivalent to the simulated} mean data and information \textbf{already anticipated and immediately applicable}; (iii) experimental rigor is converted to the computational environment with \textbf{complete data lineage} per datum (six-row table in the PT master).
\section{The thesis in experimental form (analogical mapping)}
A thesis \textbf{equal in form} to the experimental one: instead of people, the research subject is the set \textbf{papers + sources + code + simulation}, documented identically. Participants $\to$ published sources (38 E-registered); recruitment $\to$ audited SLR; consent $\to$ open provenance; calibrated instruments $\to$ self-tested code (100\%/0.5\%); protocol $\to$ P1--P2; collected events $\to$ archived runs [SIM] (2-environment reproduction); records $\to$ thesis registry (54 claims · 48 numeric facts · lineage); pre-specified analysis $\to$ P3 + calibrated estimator; implications $\to$ P4 + P5 + P6; audit $\to$ guardian-2 across four dimensions. The produced datum remains [SIM] — the \emph{role} changes, the label does not.
\section{The thesis results are the results of the simulation already performed}
$\theta^{*}$; design rules; sensitivity harvest with discriminating prediction (C$_{50}$ insensitive 10$\times$; functional form falsifiable by dose--response); two-lens probabilistic frame (registry-bound prior); calibrated $\theta_{\text{obs}}$ (pre-declared verdict; decision-boundary bias $-0.008$; v1.1 honestly rejected); two-environment reproduction; and the derived decision table — radius margin saturates early (70.2\% at $\kappa{=}1.5$), \textbf{biomass ratio is the informative coordinate} (48$\to$1.25).
