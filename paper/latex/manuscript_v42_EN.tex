\begin{abstract}\small
Prion diseases are universally fatal with no approved therapy. We report an end-to-end preclinical program converting the naturally selected human variant PrP-V127 into a quantitatively designed therapeutic platform for CJD. \textbf{Methods:} (i)~agent-assisted, provenance-verified literature audit ($\approx$90 searches; 60 findings in 9 blocks; $\sim$30 papers read in depth; 42 verified references; recurring citation errors corrected; a retracted trial excluded). (ii)~A self-tested advection--diffusion--reaction transport solver (mass conservation 100\%; Thiele error 0.5\%) yielding three falsifiable design rules: containment-ring spacing 8--12\,mm; hydrogel mesh $\xi\ge5r_p$ (HA 1--2\% passes, $>$5\% sequesters); LNP-mRNA redosing $\le$7\,d (trough 56\% of peak). (iii)~A hierarchical Bayesian frame over 10 structural analogues including the six historical antiprion failures: P(G0~go)$=$36.6\% [90\% CrI 14.6--60.5]; P(clinical slowing)$=$5.0\% [0.4--13.6] empirical \emph{vs} 30--45\% design-conditional (both lenses labeled). (iv)~A humanized in-silico infection model: the open stochastic kernel of Fornara et al.\ (iScience 2024) ported with fidelity-corrected autocatalysis ($C{\to}2C$) and fragmentation, capped by V127$\Delta$GPI as saturable substrate competition, clock-calibrated to human organoid anchors (Groveman 2019): 1 simulation unit $=$ 144 days. \textbf{Results:} containment threshold $\theta^{*}=0.333$ (front 2.83$\to$0.82\,mm at $\kappa{=}2$; near-extinction at $\kappa{=}32$) and unprompted reproduction of the MV2$>$MV1 subtype hierarchy (emergent consistency, not curve-fitting). \textbf{Pre-registered prediction (locked at release v1.0):} $\theta_{\text{measured}}<0.33$ in infected organoids $\Rightarrow$ containment in situ; readouts 90--120\,d post-seeding. \textbf{Conclusions:} every program pillar maps to an approved regulatory category (nusinersen; tofersen/NfL; 2026 Parkinsonian cell grafts; tafamidis); the program requests a conjunction of precedents, not an unprecedented category. The framework transfers as a dose-and-placement containment calculus to prion-like neurodegeneration (Alzheimer, Parkinson) --- hypothesis-generating, explicitly.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Human prion diseases are rapidly progressive and universally fatal; sporadic CJD (sCJD) survival is 6--8 months. Six candidates failed clinically (pentosan polysulfate, quinacrine, doxycycline, flupirtine, PRN100, minocycline-combinations). The G127V polymorphism of \emph{PRNP}, positively selected during the kuru epidemic, protected heterozygous carriers and confers complete resistance when homozygous in transgenic mice --- ``as protective as gene deletion'' --- with dose-dependent dominant-negative inhibition of wild-type propagation; heterozygotes remain vCJD-susceptible, mandating biallelic or anchorless designs. In 2026, recombinant anchorless V127 retained trans dominant-negative activity (with protection persisting after transgene cessation) and systemic AAV delivery of anchorless V127 extended survival $\sim$50 days in a rodent model; the structural basis is established ($\beta2$--$\alpha2$ loop restriction, intermolecular H-bond dimer stabilization). The mechanism is thus validated at four levels --- population genetics, transgenic mouse, cell culture, gene-therapy proof-of-concept. What is missing is the \emph{engineering}: delivery modality, dose, placement, timing, and a decision architecture that spends laboratory resources only where models cannot answer. This manuscript reports the complete pre-wet-lab program, fully audited.

\emph{Populations and ethics frame.} Primary target: presymptomatic genetic carriers (E200K/D178N), for whom the therapeutic window is years and autologous manufacture feasible; Brazilian E200K kindreds documented since 2007; RT-QuIC/NfL screening operational. Sporadic CJD: exclusively via an acellular LNP-mRNA vector under compassionate use, after organoid validation.

\section{Methods}
\subsection{Agent-assisted audit (provenance-verified)}
Structured blocks ($\approx$90 searches $\to$ 9 evidence tables, 60 findings; $\sim$30 papers in depth). Verification tier per source: [FT] full text extracted; [ABS] abstract; [AUDIT] bibliographic cross-check; [CODE] code inspected; [RUN] executed by us. External documents' references audited individually (one batch of 19: 11 correct, 3 duplicates, 1 non-scientific, 1 wrong link). A retracted Neurotherapeutics trial (minocycline/FK506; retraction 2020) still cited as evidence is excluded by rule; minocycline additionally confounds the NfL endpoint.

\subsection{Transport solver (WS-7)}
ADR in heterogeneous porous medium; finite volumes (2D, $192^2$, explicit Euler):
\begin{equation}
\partial(\alpha c)/\partial t=\nabla\!\cdot\!(D_{\text{eff}}\nabla c)-\nabla\!\cdot\!(\mathbf{v}c)+S(\mathbf{x})-k_{\text{eff}}c
\end{equation}
Parameters (with provenance): $\alpha{=}0.20$, $\lambda{=}1.8$ (in-vivo integrative optical imaging, Thorne \& Nicholson 2006); $D_0{=}1.25{\times}10^{-10}$\,m$^2$/s (Stokes--Einstein, $R_h{\approx}2.5$\,nm, $\sim$30\,kDa) $\Rightarrow D_{\text{eff}}{\approx}3.9{\times}10^{-11}$\,m$^2$/s; $k_{\text{eff}}$ swept $10^{-6}$--$10^{-5}$\,s$^{-1}$ (nucleated-polymerization kinetics, Masel 1999); Darcy flow with spongiform cysts at $\kappa\times50$. Self-tests: mass conservation 100.0\%; numeric vs analytic Thiele length error 0.5\%. \emph{Order-of-magnitude $\kappa$-anchoring:} a $10^5$-cell deposit at 10\,pg/cell/day gives a steady peak $\sim$0.1--1\,\textmu M within 1\,mm --- the range of half-maximal dominant-negative effect in cell culture --- so $\kappa{=}2$--4 $\leftrightarrow$ roughly 0.1--1\,\textmu M interstitial V127$\Delta$GPI (closed experimentally by arm A6).

\subsection{Bayesian frame (WS-8)}
Hierarchical Beta--Binomial over 10 analogues weighted by similarity (mechanism, vector, population, endpoint); six antiprion failures as a negative analogue; ION717 partially informative; organoid predictive validity $\approx$0.8 (PDO cohorts); $2{\times}10^5$ Monte-Carlo draws. P(G0 informative-go)$=$36.6\% [14.6--60.5]; P(clinical slowing)$=$5.0\% [0.4--13.6] empirical \emph{vs} 30--45\% design-conditional --- both lenses retained.

\subsection{Humanized in-silico model (WS-9)}
\emph{Kernel.} The stochastic reaction--diffusion model of Fornara/Igel et al.\ (iScience 2024; open code, Zenodo 11093945) ported to deterministic mean-field (96$^2$, $dt{=}5{\times}10^{-4}$). Two fidelity-critical corrections decoded from the original \texttt{findreac}: the $s{+}1$ channel is autocatalytic ($C{\to}2C$) --- the replication engine --- and the cross-reaction is fragmentation ($B_a{\to}B_{a-1}{+}C$). Mean-field unbounded autocatalysis braked by logistic saturation $C/(C+C_{50})$ (documented port limitation). \emph{Capping (this work):} freeS $=(1/(1+\kappa c_{V127}))^{2}$ enters all templating/autocatalysis terms (two-participant competitive saturation; mechanistically anchored in dose-dependent dominant-negative data). \emph{Humanization:} clock matched to human organoid anchors (Groveman 2019: clearance 25--28\,dpi; de-novo 35\,dpi; endpoint titers MV2 $2.13{\times}10^{5}$ \emph{vs} MV1 $1.69{\times}10^{3}$ SD50/mg; protease-resistant PrP only in MV2) $\Rightarrow$ human doubling $\approx$12.1\,d; 1 unit $=$ 144\,d. \emph{Humanization is a global time rescaling; relative rates remain murine} (full fitting is future work; MM1 series unpublished). MV1/MV2-like seeds differ by the 126$\times$ titer ratio. \emph{Acceptance tiers:} T1/T2 minimal screening; T3 informative success $=$ front $<$50\% baseline at $\kappa\le8$ with monotone radial gradient --- satisfied.

\subsection{Pre-registration and reproducibility}
All predictions locked at repository release v1.0 (timestamped commits) before any wet-lab data exist. Code, parameters, outputs and the executed figure (JSON+PNG of the actual run) archived in-repo. G0 gate: 8 arms (mock; disease; unedited cell; membrane-V127; \emph{secretory graft}; recombinant protein; LNP-mRNA; pentosan-polysulfate positive control), $n{=}8$/arm, primary readout proximal/distal PrP-res gradient, pre-registered go/no-go and pivot rules.

\section{Results}
\subsection{Audit} Four-layer validated core; five recurring design errors corrected (NSC$\to$microglia lineage impossibility; quiescent prion-replicating SVZ; others), each replaced by evidence-compatible branches (iPSC-microglia co-graft; slow-release deposits; LNP-mRNA).
\subsection{Design rules} R1 spacing 8--12\,mm (radius 4--6\,mm); R2 hydrogel $\xi\ge5r_p$; R3 redosing $\le$7\,d; steady state $\sim$4\,d.
\subsection{Probabilities} As \S2.3.
\subsection{Humanized thresholds} $\theta^{*}{=}0.333$; monotone to near-extinction ($\kappa{=}32$: 0.70\,mm; biomass 2.1$\times$ seed); one run $=$ 24 months tissue disease. Emergent consistency: the 126$\times$ seeding ratio reproduces MV2$>$MV1 \emph{unfitted}; because seeds differ in mass, this is hierarchy consistency, not proof of subtype-specific kinetics (same-mass control queued). \textbf{Locked prediction:} $\theta_{\text{measured}}<0.33\Rightarrow$ containment; readouts 90--120\,d.

\section{Discussion}
\emph{Contribution:} a dose-and-placement calculus no prior antiprion candidate possessed; a corrected citation record; falsifiable pre-registered predictions with a 10-month, $\sim$\$100--150k kill-or-advance assay; an executable template of agent-assisted provenance-verified translational planning. \emph{Transferability (hypothesis-generating):} Alzheimer and Parkinson spread by templated misfolding along stereotyped routes (prion-like propagation); the platform calculus is protein-agnostic; prion disease is the fastest, cheapest-to-read instance; validated containment transfers as design framework to diseases of $>$50 million people. \emph{Regulatory geometry:} chronic intrathecal redosing (nusinersen); accelerated approval by biomarker (tofersen, NfL); human brain cell grafts (2026 Parkinson trial); dominant native-state stabilization (tafamidis); substrate lowering (ASO/RNAi). The program requests the conjunction of approved categories.

\section{Limitations and gaps}
(1) $\kappa\to$concentration is order-of-magnitude only; closed by G0-A6. (2) Mean-field port omits stochastic extinction/strain diversity. (3) $C_{50}$ stability choice. (4) Anchors are MV1/MV2; MM1 unpublished; humanization $=$ global time rescale, rates murine. (5) Detection floor $10^{2}$ assumed. (6) Transport parameters healthy-tissue; synthetic cyst $\kappa\times50$. (7) Analogue weights $=$ structured judgment. (8) No wet-lab data yet --- all in-silico outputs are predictions. (9) Species/strain transfer assumed via dimensional $\theta$. (10) Immunogenicity/clearance of the anchorless construct uncharacterized (G0-A6 gives first exposure data).

\section{Ethics, governance, declarations}
Population-first design; DSMB with pre-specified stop (any confirmed teratoma); prion biosafety (single-use coaxial cannula; WHO 134\,\textdegree C/NaOH; autopsy containment); genetic privacy (LGPD); containment/slowing endpoints only. \textbf{Funding:} none (volunteer consortium). \textbf{Conflicts:} none. \textbf{AI disclosure} as title footnote. \textbf{Data/code:} everything in the public repository incl.\ claim-level traceability (33 sources E-ID; 43 claims C-ID; 23 numeric facts; four validators, zero errors).
